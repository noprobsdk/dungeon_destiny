# Hands and fingers contract

Every production humanoid profile used for visible hand posing must provide, on both hands:

- thumb: proximal, intermediate, and distal deform bones;
- index: proximal, intermediate, and distal deform bones;
- middle: proximal, intermediate, and distal deform bones;
- ring: proximal, intermediate, and distal deform bones;
- pinky: proximal, intermediate, and distal deform bones;
- anatomically placed pivots and calibrated flexion axes;
- clean weights without cross-finger bleeding;
- stable wrist/carpal transition.

Required tests are rest pose, open hand, fist, isolated joint sweeps, one-handed grip, and animation playback. Three named bones alone do not satisfy the contract.
