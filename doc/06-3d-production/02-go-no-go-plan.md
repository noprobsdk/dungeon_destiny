# 3D go/no-go plan

Before creating a broad character catalog, one vertical slice must prove:

1. one production Body Base on the target Humanoid Medium Rig Profile;
2. both hands with three deform bones per finger;
3. approved open hand, fist, and Ø29 mm one-handed grip;
4. Idle, Walk, Run, Attack, Hit, and Death without visible breakage;
5. one complete skinned outfit or armor set;
6. one separately attached one-handed weapon;
7. matching Blender, Godot, and Content Studio previews;
8. acceptable Android performance.

Failure at the agreed deadline triggers a documented fallback decision. Further speculative model generation must not replace the gate decision.
