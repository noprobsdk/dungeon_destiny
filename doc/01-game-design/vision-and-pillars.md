# Vision and pillars

Dungeon Destiny is a portrait mobile action roguelite and tactical dungeon crawler. The Hero attacks automatically while the player controls movement, positioning, Tactical Pause decisions, threat avoidance, and temporary build development.

## Pillars

1. **Clear or die:** Permanent run rewards are committed only after the final Boss is defeated and the Exit Portal is entered.
2. **Tactical control without constant micromanagement:** Automatic combat keeps movement fluid; Tactical Pause creates deliberate decision windows.
3. **Meaningful temporary builds:** Stage-end Trait choices evolve during a run and reset when the attempt ends.
4. **Permanent Hero identity:** Hero rules, Level, Ability Scores, and approved permanent progression persist between Dungeon Levels.
5. **Data-driven content:** The GDD defines behavior; Content Studio authors concrete content; Godot executes published data.
6. **Reusable 3D production:** Compatible humanoids share contracts, rigs, animation roles, sockets, and weapon families without requiring one universal body mesh.
