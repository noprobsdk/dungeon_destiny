# Dungeon Level and Stage design rules

A Dungeon Level is one finite, sequential dungeon. A Stage is one ordered encounter or progression section inside it.

Every published Dungeon Level must have:

- stable identity and sequential DL number;
- versioned name, theme, difficulty, and maze reference;
- ordered Stages;
- at least one Normal Stage and one Elite Stage before the final Boss Stage;
- exactly one final Boss Stage;
- one fixed DL XP Budget allocated across Stages;
- an Exit Portal condition after Boss defeat;
- higher configured difficulty than the previous Dungeon Level.

Concrete Dungeon Levels and Stages exist only as Content Studio records and published manifest data.
