# Encounter and balance rules

Encounter design must use approved enemy versions and centrally derived CR/Monster XP relationships. Difficulty cannot be increased through health alone; composition, movement, hazards, abilities, space, timing, and objectives must contribute.

Tactical Pause availability and expected recharge are balance inputs. Encounters must remain readable in real time and must not require Tactical Pause to compensate for unreadable telegraphs.

Trait offers are build opportunities, not substitutes for baseline encounter fairness. Teaser Traits must be evaluated separately because they can temporarily expose effects beyond the Hero's permanent progression.
