# Campaign Arc example — The Fallen Castle

> **Status:** Illustrative GDD example. It is not a live Campaign, Dungeon Arc, or Dungeon Level record until approved and authored in Content Studio.

## Arc identity

| Area | Example direction |
|---|---|
| Story | An abandoned castle is reanimating its dead inhabitants. Evidence reveals that a relic hidden beneath the royal vault is the source of the disturbance. |
| Goal | Enter the castle, discover why the dead are rising, descend into the lower levels, and recover the relic. |
| Graphic design / visual identity | Ruined gothic castle, cold stone, faded royal colors, plague markings, deepening darkness, and increasing relic energy toward the lower vaults. |
| Enemy ecosystem | Human Skeletons, Zombies, Runners, Exploders, ranged undead, necromantic support enemies, and castle guardians. |
| New tactical idea | Corpse and reanimation pressure: positioning, target priority, corpse control, and Tactical Pause assessment. |
| Climax | Reach the relic chamber and defeat the guardian while controlling reanimation and environmental relic effects. |

## Example Dungeon Level progression

| DL | Location | Gameplay identity | Story development |
|---:|---|---|---|
| 1 | Castle Courtyard | Basic melee enemies, movement, and target readability | The dead guard a castle that appears abandoned. |
| 2 | Plague Barracks | Finite Zombie Waves and Tactical Pause | The soldiers were infected and sealed inside. |
| 3 | Broken Armory | Ranged enemies, cover, and priority targets | Someone deliberately armed the undead. |
| 4 | Servants' Tunnels | Ambushes, narrow corridors, and escape routes | Survivors attempted to flee through hidden passages. |
| 5 | Desecrated Chapel | Curses and a midpoint guardian | The relic was moved beneath the chapel before the castle fell. |
| 6 | Royal Prison | Locked cells and optional tactical objectives | A prisoner or record reveals how to enter the vault. |
| 7 | Ancestral Crypts | Reanimation and corpse-control mechanics | The relic is waking the castle's ancestors. |
| 8 | Alchemist's Laboratory | Poison, experiments, and Exploders | The plague was an attempt to control the relic. |
| 9 | Sealed Vault | Elite combinations and magical seals | The guardian and the relic's true nature are revealed. |
| 10 | Relic Chamber | Multi-phase mastery examination | Defeat the guardian and recover or contain the relic. |

## Story-question chain

Each level advances a sequence rather than presenting ten unrelated missions:

```text
Why are the dead active?
→ Who sealed the barracks?
→ Who armed the dead?
→ Why was the relic moved?
→ What did the alchemists attempt?
→ What is protecting the vault?
→ What will recovering the relic unleash?
```

The concrete names, records, encounter values, enemies, story beats, and rewards remain Content Studio data after approval.
